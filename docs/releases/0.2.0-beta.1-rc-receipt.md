## Historical rc4 reviews and limitations

Fresh pre-pack correctness, simplicity, security, tests, concurrency, dependency, and packaging reviews closed every material implementation finding. The final receipt and documentation review confirmed the physical line and identified protected gates for durable preservation, CI identity, committed-source provenance, and publication.

Accepted limitations:

- Same-user processes remain inside the trust boundary; namespacing is not isolation.
- Machine-key wrapping is software-bound, not hardware attestation or theft-resistant custody.
- Approved children receive usable credentials and must be trusted.
- Output scanning ignores injected values shorter than eight characters and cannot constrain a malicious child.
- Mixed backups remain bound to the source machine; only all-interactive backups are portable.
- macOS x64 is explicitly unsupported; no Rosetta qualification is claimed.
- Windows, musl Linux, other architectures, and hardware mode are unavailable and fail closed. Hardware remains status-only.
- Source-build outputs are functionally qualified, not claimed to be byte-for-byte reproducible across compiler toolchains.
- Publication and registry smoke testing remain a later protected checkpoint requiring explicit authorization.
